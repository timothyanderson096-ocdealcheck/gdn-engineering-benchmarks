# Resolved-case calibration harness

The calibration layer replays historical evidence through the unchanged Decision Dome v0.1 engine. It measures the engine; it does not alter weights or feed outcomes back into decisions.

## Resolved-case format

`ResolvedCase` stores the original question, desired result, timeframe, sources, competing hypotheses, unknowns, chronological evidence stages, optional historically recorded conclusions, and a resolved outcome. Outcome metadata records whether the desired result was achieved, the correct hypothesis and useful actions, the earliest stage at which evidence was sufficient, information that mattered, and useful or misleading evidence and sources.

`RecordedStageConclusion` is available for importing conclusions and confidence captured by a historical system. `StageReplayResult` separately preserves the conclusion and confidence generated by the current engine, avoiding hindsight substitution.

## Measurements

- Confidence calibration uses the leading hypothesis's confidence against its observed correctness, reported as Brier score, mean bucket error, and confidence buckets.
- Stopping quality compares the first committing action with the fixture-authored earliest sufficient stage and detects later material conclusion changes.
- Information value measures rank of unknowns that mattered, top-rank hit rate, reciprocal rank, lower-ranked misses, and resolved requests that proved irrelevant.
- Source performance is grouped by source and domain. No universal source trust score is produced.
- Signal observations retain evidence type and provenance.

The fixtures are intentionally synthetic and diagnostic. Their aggregate numbers demonstrate the harness, not real-world engine quality.

Run the example with:

```sh
npx tsx examples/calibration.ts
```
