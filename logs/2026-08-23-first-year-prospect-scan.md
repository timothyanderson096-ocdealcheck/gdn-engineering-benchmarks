# GDN First-Year Prospect Scan — 2026-08-23

## Selection rule
Target very small companies founded in 2026 (therefore safely within their first year at the time of this scan), preferably 2–5 people, software-heavy, founder-reachable, and working in domains where verification matters.

For these young companies the pitch should not assume years of legacy debt. The startup offer is: **establish a verified baseline before complexity compounds**. One bounded workflow/component, one independent verification pass, measurable findings, nominal pilot scope.

## Startup-specific offer

**GDN Verification Scout — nominal pilot**

- One critical workflow/component
- Map required behaviour and evidence
- Identify redundant/unnecessary implementation complexity where present
- Run independent verification probes
- Produce concise findings and before/after evidence
- No broad rewrite and no need to expose the entire codebase

Core line remains:

**Generate less. Remove more. Verify everything.**

For first-year startups, the supporting line is:

**Build the verified baseline before complexity compounds.**
